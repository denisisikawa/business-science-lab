RevenueGuard AI — Financial Control Center

Experimental sandbox · Python controls · AI investigation planned

A fictional B2B technology provider with independent contract, service usage and billing systems. The simulator injects controlled defects; deterministic reconciliation identifies and ranks exceptions using source evidence, with separate ground truth for evaluation.

Executive summary

Expected vs. actual billing, potential unbilled revenue, excess charges and evidence-backed priorities.

Exposure heatmap & Pareto

Interactive defect × product or period matrix with absolute USD exposure, case counts and drill-down into exceptions.

Remediation plan

P1 groups cover at least 80% of absolute exposure. Suggested owners, timeboxes and acceptance criteria support triage and correction.

Reproducibility & evidence

Seeded simulator, Python reconciliation, contract-level evidence, CSV export and independent evaluation against synthetic defect labels.
